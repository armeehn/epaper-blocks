# Earthquakes

Recent notable earthquakes from the USGS GeoJSON feeds, shown as
"M 6.1 - 92 km SSW of Adak, Alaska" rows. Feed choices: significant
quakes of the past week (default), M6+ past week, or M4.5+ past day.
Quiet weeks can legitimately show an empty list. On very active days the
M4.5 daily feed may exceed the 16 KB response cap and show "response too
large" until things calm down; the default feed stays small. Suggested
size: 8x3.
